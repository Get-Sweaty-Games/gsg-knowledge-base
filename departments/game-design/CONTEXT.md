# Game Design

Unity client knowledge and game design content — systems, balance, narrative, and
Unity-technical decisions worth preserving centrally.

As this grows, natural sub-groupings (Unity-technical vs. game-design-content) may
emerge — create subfolders under `docs/` if/when that happens, each with its own
CONTEXT.md. Note the two flavors defer to different owners even though they share this
folder (see `_core/ownership-map.md`).
